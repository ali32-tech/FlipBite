Search engines no longer rank pages on keyword frequency alone. In 2026, ranking systems evaluate **semantic density** — how tightly a page's entities, relationships, and supporting concepts cluster around a single topic.

## Why semantic density matters

A page with high semantic density signals topical authority to modern retrieval systems. It tells the algorithm: this page isn't guessing at a subject, it's mapping it.

- Entity coverage across the primary topic
- Relationship strength between supporting concepts
- Consistency of terminology throughout the page

## How we measure it

Our engineering team built an internal scoring model that extracts entities from a page, maps them against a reference knowledge graph, and scores the density of meaningful connections.

Pages that score above our internal threshold consistently outperform thinner, keyword-stuffed content — even when the thinner page has more backlinks.

## What this means for content strategy

Stop writing for keywords. Start writing for entity coverage. Build content clusters where every supporting page reinforces the same core set of concepts, and let the density do the ranking work for you.
