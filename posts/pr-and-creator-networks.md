Digital PR and influencer marketing used to live in separate departments. In 2026, the highest-performing campaigns treat them as a single, coordinated function.

## Why the lines are blurring

Creator content now gets indexed, cited, and linked to at a scale that rivals traditional press placements. A single well-placed creator partnership can generate more qualified referral traffic than a dozen press mentions.

## Building a combined strategy

We now brief creators the same way we brief journalists: with a clear narrative angle, supporting data, and a natural link-back opportunity — while still respecting each platform's native format.

The result is a link and mention profile that looks organic because it is organic, built from real audience relationships instead of manufactured placements.
